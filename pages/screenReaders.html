About Screen Readers

Screen readers read a page to the user.

Built-in with Web browsers

Microsoft Edge (Microsoft Windows)

There are a couple of different ways to activate the screen reader in Microsoft Edge. The easiest, at least for me, is the keyboard shortcut Ctrl-Shift-U.

As you can see in the screenshot, Edge highlights the word it's reading in yellow, and the rest of the line in blue.

Safari

Safari for macOS

I know of only one way to activate Safari's screen reader. On the Edit menu, go down to Speech, then click on Start Speaking from the submenu. It will start reading at the top of the page, unless some text on the page is currently selected, in which case it will start at the beginning of the selection. It will continue until reaching the end of the page, the end of the selection or until you command it to Stop Speaking.
